01 / The work

Building things.
Sharing what I learn.

I run Mystic Coders, a technology consultancy that helps companies build software and navigate the ever-shifting landscape of AI, cloud, and web development. We've worked with everyone from startups to Fortune 500s.

I've written books on WebSockets and Spring. I've spoken at conferences around the world—Java2Days, ConFoo, ÜberConf, and dozens of others—about everything from real-time systems to the philosophy of software craftsmanship.
